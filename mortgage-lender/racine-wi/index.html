What is Racine's housing market like for buyers?

Racine offers more house for the money than much of metro Milwaukee: HUD's 2026 loan-limit table lists a median sale price of $260,000 for Racine County, compared with $447,000 for the Milwaukee metro counties.

Inside the city you'll find older homes near downtown and the lakefront, working-class bungalows and two-flats on the north and west sides, and some grand older homes along the lake. Mount Pleasant, Caledonia and Sturtevant add newer subdivisions just outside the city. Older city homes mean more inspection items, so budget for repairs and read what happens if the appraisal comes in low.

What down payment help can Racine buyers use?

Racine buyers' main local resource is Housing Resources Inc., a nonprofit counseling agency with a Racine office at 601 Lake Ave. Its counselors reserve grant funds for income-eligible buyers who complete homebuyer education.

  • HCRI grants — state-funded down payment and closing cost grants that HRI says are forgivable over the first three years of owner-occupancy, for purchases in Racine and nearby counties. The state's current HCRI contract period runs through September 30, 2027.
  • Downpayment Plus — Federal Home Loan Bank of Chicago grants available in every Wisconsin county through participating member institutions, forgivable over five years of owner-occupancy.
  • WHEDA — statewide first mortgages with down payment assistance. See my WHEDA page.

The City of Racine also offers a homebuyer application for city employees, and in September 2026 WPR reported a planned home development on the former Giese Elementary site with purchase assistance for City of Racine and Racine Unified employees. Grant funds are limited and change often, so check current funding availability before you write an offer that depends on them.

Which loan programs fit Racine buyers best?

FHA and conventional loans cover most Racine purchases, and the right one depends on your credit and how much you're bringing to closing.

  • FHA has flexible credit guidelines and a 2026 limit of $541,287 for one unit in Racine County, well above typical prices. See the FHA loan page.
  • Conventional loans can cost less over time for buyers with stronger credit, and mortgage insurance can be removed later. Compare them in conventional vs FHA.
  • VA loans offer some of the most flexible down payment rules available to eligible veterans and service members.
  • USDA may fit some outlying addresses in Racine County; eligibility is set by USDA's map, and I check every address.

How do property taxes and closing work in Racine?

Racine property taxes follow Wisconsin's standard calendar: billed in December, due in full by January 31 or in two installments due January 31 and July 31, and paid in arrears, so the seller credits you their share at closing.

The seller pays the state transfer fee of $3 per $1,000 of value. Grant programs add their own documents at closing, so compare your final numbers carefully against your Loan Estimate; here's how to read your Closing Disclosure.

FAQ

Questions I hear most.

What down payment assistance is available in Racine, WI?
Income-eligible Racine buyers commonly look at state-funded HCRI grants and Federal Home Loan Bank of Chicago Downpayment Plus grants, often through Housing Resources Inc.'s Racine office, plus WHEDA's statewide programs. Each has income limits and education requirements, and funding comes and goes, so confirm what's open before you write your offer.
How do I compare mortgage lenders in Racine?
Get written Loan Estimates from two or three lenders on the same day, then compare lender charges, credits and the promised closing timeline. Ask each lender which grant programs they've actually closed in Racine County and whether they'll fully underwrite your pre-approval before you write an offer. Check every loan officer on NMLS Consumer Access.
Is Racine cheaper than Milwaukee for homebuyers?
By HUD's 2026 loan-limit data, yes. HUD lists a median sale price of $260,000 for Racine County versus $447,000 for the Milwaukee metro counties. Individual neighborhoods vary widely, and older Racine homes can carry higher repair costs, so compare total ownership costs, not just price.
Can I use an FHA loan for a Racine two-flat?
Yes, if you'll live in one of the units. FHA allows owner-occupied purchases of two- to four-unit homes, and HUD's 2026 FHA limit for a two-unit property in Racine County is $693,050. Projected rent from the other unit may help you qualify within FHA's rules.
Do Racine buyers need homebuyer education for grants?
Usually, yes. Grant programs such as HCRI typically require homebuyer education or counseling through a HUD-approved agency before funds are released, and the certificate must be current at closing. Housing Resources Inc. offers classes and one-on-one counseling, and finishing it early keeps your financing timeline on track.
